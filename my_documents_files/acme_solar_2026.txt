
Acme Solar Technologies Annual Report 2026:
Executive Summary:
Acme Solar Technologies is a pioneer in high-efficiency solar panel manufacturing.
In fiscal year 2026, the company generated 128 million dollars in revenue, an increase of 35 percent compared to 2025.
The primary revenue driver was our Helios-X residential solar kit, accounting for 62 percent of total sales.
The company expanded its manufacturing facility in Austin, Texas, creating 450 new engineering jobs.
Looking ahead to 2027, Acme plans to launch commercial battery storage systems and target international markets in Europe and Japan.
